Inbound Call Script

Your live guide for answering inbound calls. Read the green SAY lines aloud, word-for-word if you like; the gray italic notes are coaching for you only — don't read those. The script fills itself in as you type.

How to use it
  1. Collect the caller up top — first/last name, phone, email, address. Tap ⌂ sq ft to look up the home size, and Save to GHL to push the contact into GoHighLevel.
  2. Pick the service tab — House Cleaning, Window Cleaning, Airbnb/Turnover, Pressure Washing, or Commercial/HOA.
  3. Enter the square footage in the right-hand panel — pricing updates instantly from our range buckets (House & Window quote live; the others collect details for the owner to follow up).
  4. Tap the pain points in Discovery, or type a phrase — the tailored closing paragraph in step 4 rewrites itself for you to read right before pricing.
  5. Set the appointment day & arrival window in the panel — it fills into your scheduling and close lines.
  6. Log the outcome at the bottom: Declined, Maybe (sends an estimate), or Booked (kicks off the Needs-Scheduled cadence).

Tip: sections marked with a ▸ arrow are optional branches — open them only when the scenario fits.

1 · Verify the home

2 · Discovery — find the real reason & what it's costing them

This is where the sale is made. If they haven't said why they're calling:

What made you decide to get the home cleaned right now?

Dig into the consequence — let them feel the cost (this first line adapts to the reasons you picked):

If it stayed the way it is now, how would you feel walking in a week from now — is that something you want to keep dealing with?
How long has this been on your list that you keep meaning to get to?
What have you already tried?
▸ If they used a cleaning company before
What did you like, and what would you want done differently this time?
▸ If they tried doing it themselves
Why do you think that hasn't stuck?
▸ If they only want a partial clean (bathrooms / floors / baseboards)

Don't lock into the small job. Give them a judgment-free way to admit the rest slips:

Ok, it sounds like you just want a few areas cleaned — that makes sense. And the rest — the dusting, cabinets, baseboards — are you keeping up with that yourself, or is that the kind of thing that tends to pile up?

If they admit it's slipping, go to the widening trial close:

If we could just keep the whole home clean for you, would that take a weight off your shoulders?

If they hold firm on two rooms, book the two rooms and earn the rest later. Never push past a clear no.

What's your favorite room — where do you spend the most time?
And what's the one thing we could take care of that would make this a 5-star experience for you?

Offer examples only if they stall: pet hair, soap buildup in the shower, grease in the oven. Both answers feed the tailored closing.

3 · Present value as questions, not a speech
Sounds good. Let me put your estimate together. While I do, can I ask a couple quick things so I know we're the right fit?

Turn each strength into a question tied to what they told you — they'll sell themselves:

Would it be more convenient to have a team of two or three in and out efficiently, rather than one person there most of the day?
How important is it that everyone who enters your home is background checked, and that we're licensed, bonded, and insured?
That's exactly how we run every job — we hold our team to a high standard, and we'll clean your home top to bottom, ceiling fans down to the baseboards and floors, every visit.

Know your three packages cold

Gold (most complete): interior + exterior, screens removed & cleaned, tracks, sills & frames, PLUS whole-house soft wash.
Silver (most popular): interior + exterior, screens cleaned, sills & frames. No tracks/soft wash.
Bronze (curb view): exterior windows + screens only.
Minimum job is $197. Crew of one or two. If asked how long: "a few factors affect it, plan for at least a few hours." Never give a firm time.
