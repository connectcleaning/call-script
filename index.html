Don't lock into the small job. Give them a judgment-free way to admit the rest slips:

Makes sense on the bathrooms and floors. And the rest — the dusting, cabinets, baseboards — are you keeping up with that yourself, or is that the kind of thing that tends to pile up?

If they admit it's slipping, go to the widening trial close:

If we could just keep the whole home clean for you, would that take a weight off your shoulders?

If they hold firm on two rooms, book the two rooms and earn the rest later. Never push past a clear no.

What's the one thing we could take care of that would make this a 5-star experience for you?

Offer examples only if they stall: pet hair, soap buildup in the shower, grease in the oven.
